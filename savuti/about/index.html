About Us

Founded in 2013, Savuti Capital provides capital raising, investor relations and strategic advisory services from our headquarters in the UK (London) and in the EU (Lisbon).

We consistently deliver value for our clients by leveraging our global network of institutional investor relationships, principally in the UK, Switzerland and across the EU.

Our clients are outstanding asset management firms in the US, Europe and Asia, active in hedge funds, Private Credit, Private Equity and infrastructure (including primary funds, secondaries and co-investments).

Savuti Capital combines experienced leadership and a dedicated middle-office team with advanced tech and data resources, enabling deep-dive research, timely communications with prospects, and reporting on engagement metrics and relationship progression.

Savuti Capital: what’s in the name?

Savuti, in Botswana, boasts one of the greatest concentrations of animals in Southern Africa and is famous for its mysterious river channel. Flowing during the life of Dr David Livingstone (a distant relation of our Founder), the Savuti channel ran dry after his death but has flowed again since the mid-20th century, a fitting metaphor for the welcome stream of capital that follows a successful fundraise.
