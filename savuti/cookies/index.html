Cookies Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit our website. They help us make the site work better for you.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Understand how you use our website
  • Remember your preferences
  • Improve your experience
  • Ensure the website's security

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function
  • Analytical cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Performance cookies: Help us improve website performance

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can delete existing cookies and set your browser to prevent new ones from being placed.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at info@savuticapital.com.
