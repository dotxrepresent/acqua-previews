Leadership Team

Ben Scales

Founder & Managing Director · London

Ben founded Savuti Capital in 2013. Ben has over 20 years' experience in the alternative investment industry at leading firms, such as Man Group, in the UK, France and Switzerland. Ben has worked in institutional sales since 2008. Ben attained the CAIA designation in 2014. He qualified as a UK lawyer in 2000 and holds a BA (2.1 Hons) degree from Durham University. Ben speaks fluent French, German and Spanish.

David Chiene

COO · London

David has worked in financial services for 20 years. He has held leadership roles at a Tier 1 global investment bank in functions ranging from operations and business management to finance and data. David was Finance Business Partner for the largest operations divisions in the bank and developed one of the bank's key strategic data services to optimise regulatory compliance. David has qualifications in project management and accounting, and holds a BA (2.1 Hons) degree from Bristol University.

Reuben Maltby

Director · Lisbon

Reuben has extensive investment experience across real estate and the electricity, carbon, gas, and base metals markets. He founded and sold a company focused on carbon emissions and renewable energy, arranging equity for renewable projects and raising substantial capital from LPs. He holds an MSc in Trade & Finance from City St George's, University of London, and a BSc (Joint Hons) from Bristol University.

Martine van Oosterum

Director · Amsterdam

Martine has 20 years' experience in wealth management and consulting. She has held senior roles in private banking at Credit Suisse and ABN AMRO in the UK and Spain and as a Management Consultant at Ernst & Young in the Netherlands. Martine has also worked in business development for asset management start-ups. She is a co-founder of Women In Digital Assets. Martine was a client of Savuti Capital with her previous firm.

Nicolas Firzli

Advisory Board Chair · Paris

Nicolas has extensive experience in asset management and investment policy across private equity, infrastructure, tech, defence, and energy. He has advised leading public pension funds, endowments, and supranational institutions, including the World Bank Group, and held senior business development roles at AXA Investment, BNP Paribas, and Japan's SPARX Group. He holds degrees from McGill University (BA Stats), HEC School of Management (MBA), and the University of Paris Law School (LL.M.). Nicolas founded and co-chairs the World Pensions® Forum and regularly contributes to discussions on pension investments and private markets for BBC News, Bloomberg, RTÉ Ireland, and Asharq Business News.
